Orchestry - Senior Product Manager: application answers (draft, Kfir to approve)

1. What attracted you to Orchestry and this role?

The roles and permissions rebuild was the hardest problem I worked on at Bewith, and I enjoyed it a lot. It let clients expand contracts 100%+ into new departments. Orchestry works on that same problem at much larger scale, inside the Microsoft 365 tools I already use, like Teams and SharePoint, for IT admins. I worked directly with city IT teams as their main technical contact, and I want to keep building for that kind of user.

2. A product decision without perfect information

Clients asked us for "recurring payments." I had no proof of what that really meant, and Sales, CS and Engineering each wanted something different. Before writing a spec, I registered on a competitor's platform and found a full entitlement system behind its simple subscriptions. That changed the scope from a payment feature to entitlements plus an access-control rebuild. I got Sales, CS and Engineering to agree on explicit guardrails, and we shipped that scope. I validated it by results: $100K+ in incremental ARR, from 3 new clients and 4 expansions.

3. Time with customers and an insight that changed a product problem

I talked to customers in requirements sessions, on-site visits, roadmap reviews with city managers, client calls with CS, and interviews and usability tests with residents. One insight: clients were copy-pasting event descriptions from their own documents into our event form. I read that as a behavior, not a request, because nobody had asked us to change the form. It told me the form was the problem, not the clients. I redesigned it as a step-by-step flow and A/B tested it against the original. Time to first event dropped from tens of minutes to a few minutes.
