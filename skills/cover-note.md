# Skill: cover note

Only when the application requires one or the job is a top pick.

## Shape (3–4 sentences)
1. Open plainly: "I'm applying for the <Role> role."
2. One concrete story from `career-context.md` §4 that matches the posting.
3. Why the role fits (a fact, not a feeling).
4. Close on something concrete from the posting.

## Don'ts
- No lines echoing the company's mission back at them — they read as AI-written. Cut every one.
- Banned words and voice rules from `career-context.md` apply.

Save as `jobs/<folder>/cover-note.txt` and paste it in chat for copying.
