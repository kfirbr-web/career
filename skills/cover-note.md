# Skill: cover note

Only when the application requires one, the job is a top pick, or Kfir asks.

## Shape (Kfir's model, 2026-09-30; ~250-350 words, 5-6 short paragraphs)
0. **Header:** `Kfir Braunstein` / `Montreal, QC, Canada  |  514-462-2234  |  kfirbr@gmail.com`
   (the facts-file phone; 431-294-5256 is old).
1. **Opening point (1-2 sentences):** a real lesson from Kfir's five years that matters for this job.
   No "I'm applying for…" opener.
2. **Why this posting:** react to specific things the JD says about the work (its named problems,
   how it describes the role, what it warns you about) and say why they match what Kfir has run into.
   Quote or name the JD's own points; don't paraphrase the company's mission or values.
3. **Proof:** what Kfir owned end to end at Bewith that maps to the role, in plain nouns (systems,
   processes, decisions), with 1-3 metrics from the facts file.
4. **Personal angle (optional):** a confirmed personal fact that connects to the domain (e.g. hiking
   and daily training for health/fitness; the architecture degree for built-world companies).
   Only facts in `career-context.md`.
5. **Close:** the match in one or two sentences, and a plain line of interest.
6. **Sign-off:** `Kfir Braunstein`
- Honest gap line (e.g. French A2) only when the JD makes it a must-have; one sentence, no apology.

## Don'ts
- No echoing the company's mission or values back at them; engage with the work the JD describes.
- No "not X, it's Y" contrasts, no filler adjectives, no em dashes.
- Banned words and voice rules from `career-context.md` apply; every claim traces to the facts file.
